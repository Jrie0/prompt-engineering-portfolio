 [Prompt Name] Product Pitch Generator
 
## Overview
**Purpose:** Turn product specs into a 30 second sales pitch.
**Structure:** RTF with constraints.
**Technique:** Zero-shot
---
## The Prompt

**Role:**
Act as a pro salesman who writes very persuasive elevator pitches.
**Task:**
Take the product details and target audience and turn it into a 30 second elevator pitch.
**Contraints:**
Limit the entire pitch to 75-90 words.
Don't use corporate slop words like revolutionary or next gen.
**Format:**
The Hook: Sentence pointing out the customers struggle.
The solution: 2 sentences introducing the product and how it eases the customers struggle.
The Advantage: A sentence explaining why the product is better than alternatives.
Call to action: A closing sentence leading the customer on what to do next.
## Context and Inputs
List the information the user has to supply, written as placeholders:
- **[Product Details]:** The main specifications and benefits of the product being pitched.
- **[Target Audience]:** The group of people the pitch is made for.

---
## Output Requirements
**Format:** Four numbered sections (hook, solution, advantage, call to action) under 90 words total around 30 second speech.
**Constraints:** No unfitting words so it is smooth when spoken.
**Tone and Style:** Persuasive, Direct, Professional.
