# Business Prompt: Product Marketing Pitch Generator

## Context and Inputs
List the information the user has to supply, written as placeholders:
- **[PRODUCT_NAME]:** The name of the product or service being promoted.
- **[TARGET_AUDIENCE]:** The specific customer group or demographic.
- **[KEY_BENEFIT]:** The main feature or problem the product solves.
- **[TONE]:** The desired brand voice (e.g., energetic, professional, playful).

---

## Output Requirements
**Format:** A 3-paragraph marketing pitch organized as follows:
- **Paragraph 1 (The Hook):** Introduce a common problem or pain point faced by the target audience.
- **Paragraph 2 (The Solution):** Introduce the product and explain how its key benefit solves the problem.
- **Paragraph 3 (Call to Action):** End with a clear, compelling call to action.

**Constraints:** 
- Keep the total output under 250 words.
- Do NOT use generic hype or overused buzzwords (e.g., "game-changer," "revolutionary," "next-level").
- Do NOT make unverified health or financial claims.

**Tone and Style:** Energetic, persuasive, and professional.

---

## Role & Instructions
You are an expert product marketing strategist. Use the provided inputs to draft a high-converting product pitch that strictly follows the format, constraints, and tone requirements outlined above.

## Prompt Template
```text
Role: You are an expert product marketing strategist specializing in direct-to-consumer physical products.

Task: Draft a concise, high-converting product marketing pitch based on the provided input details.

Inputs:
- Product Name: [PRODUCT_NAME]
- Target Audience: [TARGET_AUDIENCE]
- Key Benefit: [KEY_BENEFIT]
- Tone: [TONE]

Constraints:
- Keep the pitch under 250 words total across 3 short paragraphs.
- Avoid overused buzzwords like "game-changer," "revolutionary," or "next-level."
- Do not make unverified claims.

Format:
1. Paragraph 1 (The Hook): Identify a common frustration or pain point.
2. Paragraph 2 (The Solution): Introduce the product and key feature.
3. Paragraph 3 (Call to Action): End with a clear next step for the customer.
